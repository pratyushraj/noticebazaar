Frequently Asked Questions

What is a collaboration agreement?

A collaboration agreement is a legal contract between a brand and an influencer/creator that outlines the terms of their partnership, including deliverables, payment, content usage rights, and other important terms. It protects both parties and ensures clarity.

What's the difference between paid and barter collaborations?

Paid collaborations involve monetary compensation, while barter collaborations involve product or service exchange. Our generator supports both types and can handle hybrid agreements that include both payment and product exchange.
